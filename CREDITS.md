# Photography credits

Every photograph on this site is a **placeholder**. None of it shows The Cliffs.

All images come from Wikimedia Commons. Most are licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**, which requires
visible attribution wherever the image is published. That is why the site footer links here.

**When real photography of The Cliffs replaces these files, delete this file and remove the
footer link.** Until then it must stay reachable — it is the licence condition, not a courtesy.

---

## ⚠️ Read this before launch

The three suite photographs and five gallery photographs are of **Amantaka, a real hotel in
Luang Prabang, Laos**, photographed by Basile Morin. They were chosen because they are the only
coherent, high-quality luxury interiors on Commons and they match the palette almost exactly.

They are legally licensed and correctly attributed here. They are still pictures of **somebody
else's hotel on another continent**. Publishing them as though they were The Cliffs would
misrepresent the property to people about to spend money. Replace them first.
