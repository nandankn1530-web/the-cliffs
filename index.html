960 M  ·  The arrival

The road runs out before the view does.

You leave the highway at a junction with no sign worth reading, and for the next hour the road does nothing but climb. Coffee under silver oak. Then cardamom. Then nothing planted at all.

Somewhere around the ninth switchback the light changes and you realise you have driven up through the cloud rather than under it. The valley you were looking at is now beneath you, and what is above you is entirely sky.

1240 M  ·  Days here

What the day does to you here.

Nothing is scheduled. This is roughly what happens anyway.

  • 06:10

    Coffee before the cloud lifts

    Filter kaapi on the veranda while the valley is still filling in. It burns off by seven.

  • 07:30

    The forest wakes up loud

    Malabar trogon, hornbill, whistling thrush. Bring binoculars; you will not need a guide.

  • 10:00

    Walk out along the ridge

    An estate track follows the contour for four kilometres. No gates, no ticket, no one else.

  • 15:00

    Down to the water

    Forty minutes down through shola forest to a pool cold enough to make you shout.

  • 18:45

    Then nothing at all

    A treatment can be arranged in the room. Most people just sit outside until it is dark.

1400 M  ·  Questions

Before you book.

How do we actually get there?

Drive, or be driven. It is about five and a half hours from Bengaluru and three and a half from Mangaluru airport. The last hour is a single lane of switchbacks — fine in any car, slow in the rain. We will send a pin and a written description of the final turning, which is easy to miss.

When should we come?

October to March is clear, cold at night, and the views go on for fifty kilometres. June to September is the monsoon — far greener, far louder, and the cloud sits in the room with you. Both are worth doing; they are not the same holiday.

What is included?

Breakfast, housekeeping, and someone on the property who can cook if you would rather not. Estate walks and the waterfall track cost nothing. The full current list lives on the Airbnb listing, which is the version that stays up to date.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights. One night is technically possible and genuinely not worth it — you would spend most of it driving, and the whole point of the place is the second morning.

What if our plans change?

Cancellation runs through Airbnb under the policy shown on the listing, so you are covered by their terms rather than ours. Check the exact window on the booking page before you confirm.

Children and dogs?

Children yes, and the Long Room is built for them. Dogs by arrangement. Do bear in mind there is an unfenced drop at the end of the lawn — it is a spectacular garden and not a childproof one.
